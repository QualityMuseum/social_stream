Anleitungen / Monetarisierung

Run a card break

Keep buyer assignments on screen while you open cards, then export a recap for sorting.

Start your first break

  1. Turn on Social Stream and enable remote API control in its settings. Open Monetization > Open Breaks. This opens your private workspace link.
  2. Wähle Set up. Select a sports preset, use numbered spots, or paste one spot name per line. Spreadsheet columns can be pasted in this order: name, asking price, description, private note.
  3. Enter a title, selling platform, and currency, then choose Start break.
  4. In Run break, select a spot, enter its buyer, and choose Save spot. A buyer name claims an available spot.
  5. Öffne den Abschnitt OBS and audience tools, copy the OBS overlay link, and add it as a Browser Source. Use the workspace for controls and the overlay link for viewers.

Preview a sample board

Keep the private link private

Breaks has two links. The OBS overlay link shows viewers the public board. The private control link opens this workspace, with its payments, notes, costs and stock. Use it for your own windows and OBS docks, and never as a Browser Source or in a chat.

Breaks records are encrypted between the workspace and your SSN host, using a key that only the private link holds. Your session ID cannot read or change them, and the key is never sent over the network. The overlay is different: like every SSN overlay, it trusts its session, so someone with your session ID could send it false content. They still cannot see or change your records. If the private link leaks, choose Monetization > Reset private link. Every workspace using the old link locks and clears its screen.

Encryption needs HTTPS, localhost or the app. A workspace opened from a plain HTTP address on your network cannot connect.

During the break

Verwende Highlight to call attention to a spot. Enter a pull and choose Aufdecken to show it. Card image URLs go under Description and private note. To free a sold spot, set it to Verfügbar and save: this clears its buyer, payment and pull, and the activity log keeps who held it.

Payment and price holds your asking price, confirmed paid amount, and payment status. Check payment on your selling platform before manually marking a spot paid.

Swap buyers exchanges owners, and each buyer keeps their payment record and private note. Undo reverses the last spot change, and hovering over it shows what that was; it leaves stage, visibility, lock and appearance alone. Lock prevents assignment edits until unlocked.

Add spots while filling. Only empty, available spots can be removed. Stage and visibility controls affect the Breaks display; they do not change your selling platform or OBS scenes.

Verwende Set up for grid, compact-strip, or side-column layouts, colors, and optional buyer names or asking prices. Available-only display hides taken spots without removing their records.

Finish and reuse

Export CSV downloads assignments grouped by buyer, including payment amounts and private notes. Keep this file for your own sorting records.

Archive finishes the break and hides its board. Starting another break also offers to archive the current one; a break nothing has happened on is discarded instead of saved. History keeps up to 30 breaks. When it is full, use Export full record to keep everything a break holds, including stock records, costs and activity, then remove it. The CSV only covers spots. Each break keeps its latest 500 activity entries. Everything has to fit through the session relay, so very long lists of notes, saved setups or stock can reach a limit; removing some makes room.

Save up to 12 setups. Setups preserve spot names, asking prices, images, and appearance; buyers, payment records, results, and private notes are cleared. Use current setup prepares a fresh copy of the current spot list.

Stock and costs

Stock keeps up to 100 local items with quantities and unit costs. It is optional and only there for breaks: it does not handle shipping or orders. Set up break copies an item's name and currency into the new-break form; it does not reduce stock.

To open something from stock, use Stock used unter Run break, oder Use 1 in this break in Stock. This reduces the local quantity and records the item, quantity and unit cost on the break. Return to stock reverses it, including from a saved break under History. Undo does not touch stock, and a removed item comes back when returned.

If you already connected an eBay seller account in Monetization, the workspace shows its cached listings. Copy a listing into a stock draft, review quantity, and enter your own cost. Listing prices are sale prices. Local edits do not update eBay listings or stock.

Whatnot, eBay Live, TikTok, and other shows can use manual assignments. Breaks does not automatically match purchases, randomizer results, payments, or refunds to spots.

The private estimate uses host-confirmed paid amounts, product cost, stock used, and fees you enter. All amounts use the break's currency; stock with no unit cost or in another currency is listed as not counted, and no exchange-rate conversion is applied.

Optional controls and games

Links under OBS and audience tools open existing games, polls, timers, and waitlists with your session. Keep audience games separate from purchase assignments and use formats allowed by your selling platform.

Der Event-Flow- Commerce control action can highlight a spot, change the stage, and show or hide the overlay. It cannot read Breaks records or change buyers, payments, costs, or what the overlay shows publicly, because flows can be started through your session. Use "boardId":"active" so a flow keeps working from break to break.

{"boardId":"active","id":"1"}

Use that example with breakHighlight. Für breakStage, verwende stage mit filling, breaking, oder finished. Für breakSettings, verwende visible mit true oder false. Use existing Flow Actions for sounds and celebrations.

Remote commands

The session API does not accept Breaks commands, because the session ID also appears in overlay links.

Records stay on the SSN installation hosting the session. Keep that host running for controls and overlays. Export completed breaks before changing installations or clearing app data. The workspace contains private records; do not use its URL as a viewer overlay.
